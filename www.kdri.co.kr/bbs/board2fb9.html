Recruitment Notice for a Cosmetic Skin Irritation Test


We are recruiting participants for a study evaluating the skin irritation potential of a cosmetic product. Both males and females aged 20 to 59 years are eligible to apply.

In this study, patches containing cosmetic products will be applied to the back for 24 hours, and irritation responses will be observed over the following 3 days.

The irritation response is generally mild and does not involve serious risks or significant adverse effects. Typical reactions may include slight itching and mild redness (erythema).

﻿* Compensation will not be provided if you withdraw from the study before completion.

 

1. Eligibility Criteria

- Males and females aged 20 to 59 years, West Asia

- Not currently participating in another back patch test study

- Able to visit the study site on 4 consecutive days

- Do not have sensitive skin

- Foreign nationals may participate if they meet the following requirements :


<Eligibility Requirements for Foreign Participants>

- Legally residing foreign nationals aged 20 to 59 years (must possess a valid visa and Alien Registration Card)

- Holders of tourist visas (B-2) are not eligible

- Must have a Korean bank account under their own name that allows deposits and withdrawals


 

2. Test Area

- Back

*Individuals with significant scarring (e.g., acne scars, sun damage marks) or hyperpigmentation on the back may not be eligible to participate.

 


3. Study Period and Visit Schedule (4 Visits)

June 9 (Day 1 / Start)

June 10 (Day 2)

June 11 (Day 3)

June 12 (Day 4 / End)

- Estimated duration per visit: approximately 1 hour on each visit day

​ 

 

4. Precautions

- Visits on Day 1 and Day 2 must be conducted at the same time of day.

- Showering and exercising are prohibited on Day 1. (Six to eight patches measuring 5 cm × 12 cm will be attached to the back.)

- From Day 2 through Day 4, participants must wear dark-colored tops. (Test areas will be marked on the back using a skin marker.)

- Participants may be excluded if, upon examination, excessive scarring or hyperpigmentation on the back is determined to interfere with study participation.

- Participants will be withdrawn from the study if a patch falls off or if the marked test areas become erased during the study period, even if this occurs on the final visit day.


 

5. Participation Compensation

- KRW 70,000 (upon completion of all 4 visits)

- Compensation will be paid via bank transfer to the participant's personal bank account on the following Thursday after study completion.


 


Important Notices (Please Read Carefully)

★No transportation reimbursement will be provided if participation is canceled due to personal negligence or personal reasons, either before the first visit or during the study.★  

★In the event of withdrawal due to unavoidable circumstances, transportation reimbursement will be provided at KRW 5,000 per completed visit.★ 

★Participants who arrive late due to personal reasons will not be permitted to participate in the study.★ 

★Simultaneous participation in studies conducted by other clinical research institutions is strictly prohibited. (Violation may result in exclusion without reimbursement. Concurrent participation may affect study results and make it difficult to identify the cause of any adverse reactions.)★  

★Visit times may be adjusted due to internal scheduling requirements.★ 

★Parking is not available within the building. Please use public parking facilities or public transportation.★

★Completion compensation will be processed sequentially beginning on the Thursday of the week following the study completion date.★
