KMTR Successfully Concludes Participation in in-cosmetics Korea 2026

페이지 정보

작성자 admin 댓글 0 조회 3,158 작성일 26-07-16 17:39

본문

The Korea Material Testing & Research Institute (KMTR) successfully concluded its participation in in-cosmetics Korea 2026,
engaging in active technical exchanges and business meetings with cosmetics and biotechnology companies from Korea and around the world.

At this year's exhibition, KMTR showcased its latest clinical evaluation services and analytical solutions for scientifically
validating cosmetic efficacy. Visitors showed great interest in KMTR's advanced evaluation technologies,
which provide objective and quantitative evidence of product performance.

[ Featured Evaluation Technologies ]

■ Triple Comedone Evaluation
This evaluation simultaneously analyzes Porphyrins, Blackheads, and Whiteheads to quantitatively assess pore condition
and sebum-related improvements. The technology attracted significant interest as an objective evaluation solution for acne-care and pore-care products.

■ Multi-area 3D Facial Volume Assessment
Utilizing 360° 3D facial imaging technology, this evaluation quantitatively measures volume changes across multiple facial regions,
providing scientific evidence for the efficacy of lifting, firming, and anti-aging products.

■ Soft Focus Effect Evaluation
This evaluation objectively measures the visual skin-enhancing effects of makeup base and tone-up products by analyzing
optical changes on the skin surface and improvements in skin texture. It received strong interest from color cosmetics and base makeup brands.

■ Cellular Senescence-Based Anti-aging Evaluation
KMTR introduced its next-generation anti-aging evaluation platform utilizing cellular senescence biomarkers such as SA-β-gal,
providing innovative efficacy assessment methods for functional cosmetic development.

■ 2-Nonenal Cleansing Efficacy Evaluation
Using 2-Nonenal, the primary compound associated with aging body odor, as a target marker, this evaluation objectively
measures cleansing efficacy before and after product use through LC-MS/MS analysis. The technology received considerable attention from visitors.

■ Skin Microbiome Analysis & Balance Care Technology
KMTR also introduced its advanced skin microbiome analysis platform, designed to evaluate skin health and microbiome balance,
highlighting next-generation technologies for skincare research.

 Through this exhibition, KMTR expanded opportunities for collaboration with both domestic and international cosmetics companies
while further demonstrating its expertise as a leading clinical research institute specializing in cosmetic efficacy evaluation for the global market.

 KMTR also introduced its multi-ethnic subject recruitment network and newly developed evaluation platforms, attracting strong interest from companies preparing to enter overseas markets and seeking globally applicable clinical studies.

 Moving forward, KMTR will continue to provide the optimal solutions for enhancing product competitiveness through cutting-edge dermatological research and objective efficacy evaluation technologies.
