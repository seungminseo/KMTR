Korea Material Testing & Research Institute (KMTR) has signed an MOU with a Chinese partner institution to
facilitate human application testing for Chinese participants and assist in the registration of cosmetic hygiene permits in China.

Shanghai Control Testing Technology Co., Ltd. is a specialized cosmetic efficacy evaluation and
registration agency that provides technical support for cosmetic companies.

The institution is dedicated to scientific evaluation of cosmetic ingredients and products, including:
Safety Evaluation, Efficacy Evaluation, Consumer Testing. Through these comprehensive assessments,
we offer technical support and valuable insights to help clients enhance product quality and create value in the Chinese market.

Definition Products with special functions such as hair dye, perm, freckle removal, whitening, UV protection, hair loss prevention, and new functional claims General cosmetics excluding special cosmetics
Related Institution National Medical Products Administration (NMPA) Local Drug Administration where the domestic responsible person is located
Approval Process Approval System : License issued after review Registration System : Pre-registration followed by review
Registration & Approval Process
  • 01
    Company Information Registration

    Appoint a domestic responsible person in China Sign an authorization agreement Register the company with NMPA and apply for a system account

  • 02
    Product Review Stage

    Formulation inspection Outer packaging & claim verification Submission and verification of required documents Preparation of test samples

  • 03
    Testing & Inspection Stage

    Basic tests (Microbial, Heavy Metal, Physicochemical, Toxicity Evaluation, etc.) Clinical trials & efficacy testing Receive test reports

  • 04
    Technical Review Stage

    Evaluation based on product category (Special or Non-Special Cosmetic)

  • 05
    Registration & License Issuance

    For non-special cosmetics → Pre-registration followed by review For special cosmetics → Approval granted after review & issuance of license
