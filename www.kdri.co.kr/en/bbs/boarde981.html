UV Protection Evaluation – Special Promotion

KMTR (Korea Material Testing & Research Institute) offers comprehensive UV protection testing services in accordance with global ISO standards.
From SPF to PA, Hybrid SPF, and Water Resistance, we provide reliable and scientifically validated evaluation solutions to support
your product development and global market entry.

Our Testing Services

✔ SPF (ISO 24444:2019, In Vivo)
Gold-standard SPF evaluation through controlled human testing.

✔ SPF (ISO 23675:2024, In Vitro)
Highly reproducible SPF assessment using advanced robotic application technology.

✔ PA (ISO 24442:2022, In Vivo)
Reliable UVA protection evaluation based on Persistent Pigment Darkening (PPD).

✔ PA (ISO 24443:2021, In Vitro)
Efficient in vitro UVA protection screening for rapid performance assessment.

✔ Hybrid SPF (ISO 23698:2024)
Next-generation evaluation combining in vivo UVA and in vitro UVB testing.

✔ Water Resistance (ISO 16217:2020 / ISO 18861:2020)
Assessment of SPF retention after water exposure (40/80 minutes).


Why KMTR ?
- Multi-ethnic human clinical trial pool available.
- Expertise in global regulatory-compliant testing.
- Fast, reliable, and reproducible results.
- Customized study design tailored to your product needs.
