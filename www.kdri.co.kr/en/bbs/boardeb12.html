April Special Promotion: Scalp & Hair Clinical Evaluation

At KMTR, we are excited to introduce our April promotion
focused on scalp and hair efficacy evaluation.

In today’s competitive global market,
product claims must be supported by reliable clinical data.
KMTR offers multi-ethnic clinical trials designed to generate
globally acceptable efficacy results.

This promotion includes comprehensive evaluations such as:

✔ Scalp hydration and sebum balance
✔ Scalp sensitivity and microcirculation
✔ Hair shine, thickness, and strength
✔ Hair shedding reduction

Additionally, safety and compatibility tests such as
HRIPT and patch tests are available to ensure product reliability.

If you are preparing for global expansion or need
strong clinical evidence for your product,
this is the perfect opportunity.
