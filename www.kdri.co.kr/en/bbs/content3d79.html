Privacy Policy

Initial Announcement Date: August 1, 2018

This policy outlines the standards and procedures for handling personal information at Korea Dermatology Research Institute (KMTR).

Chapter 1: General Provisions

Article 1 (Purpose)

This regulation establishes the standards and procedures for handling personal information at Korea Dermatology Research Institute (KMTR).

Article 2 (Scope of Application)

This regulation applies to all personal information-related tasks carried out by KMTR.

Article 3 (Definitions)

"Personal Information" – Any information related to a living individual that can be used to identify them, including name, resident registration number, video data, etc. Even if the information alone does not identify an individual, it is considered personal information if it can be combined with other data to do so.

"Processing" – Includes the collection, creation, linking, recording, storage, retention, editing, retrieval, output, correction, restoration, use, disclosure, deletion, and other similar activities related to personal information.

"Personal Information File" – A structured set of personal data arranged systematically for easy searchability.

"CCTV (Closed-Circuit Television)" – Any device used for continuous video surveillance, including:

Cameras installed in a fixed location to capture images and transmit them through wired or wireless closed networks.

Devices used to record or store captured video.

"Data Subject" – The individual identified by the processed personal information.

Article 4 (Relationship with Other Regulations)

If there are separate regulations regarding computerized personal information processing, those specific regulations take precedence over this policy.

Article 5: Personal Information Protection Organization

KMTR has appointed key personnel to oversee personal data management:

Chief Privacy Officer (CPO):

Responsible for overall coordination and supervision of personal information protection efforts.

Departmental Privacy Officers:

Responsible for implementing personal information protection in their respective departments.

Key responsibilities include:

Establishing and implementing privacy protection plans.

Responding to privacy breaches.

Monitoring and managing personal information processing activities.

Conducting privacy training.

Ensuring system integration related to data processing.

Article 6: Personal Information Handlers

Employees handling personal information are designated as Personal Information Handlers and perform the following duties:

Processing customer inquiries and administrative services that involve personal data.

Managing personal information files, including viewing, editing, and deleting records.

Administering KMTR’s website.

Handling CCTV surveillance data.

Managing internal employee records.

Installing and maintaining security programs to protect personal data.

Personal Information Handlers must NOT:

Access personal information without necessity.

Modify, damage, or disclose personal data.

All handlers must complete at least one privacy training session per year.

Article 7: Principles of Personal Information Protection

KMTR must clearly define the purpose of collecting personal information and collect only the minimum amount necessary.

Personal data must be accurate and up-to-date while preventing unauthorized modifications.

KMTR must publicly disclose its policies regarding personal information handling and ensure data subjects' rights (e.g., the right to access their data).

Article 8: Disclosure of Privacy Regulations

If KMTR operates a corporate website or departmental web pages, its privacy policies must be posted online for easy access.

If KMTR modifies its privacy policy, it must clearly disclose the changes before implementation, ensuring that data subjects can compare the previous and updated versions.

Chapter 2: Collection and Processing of Personal Information

Article 9: Collection and Use of Personal Information

KMTR may collect and use personal data only in the following cases:

✔ With explicit prior consent from the data subject.

✔ When required by law.

✔ If it is necessary to perform legal duties and cannot be accomplished otherwise.

✔ If essential for fulfilling contractual obligations.

✔ To prevent urgent threats to life, safety, or property when obtaining prior consent is impossible.

✔ When collecting personal data from publicly available sources such as business cards or online platforms, within the scope of reasonable expectations.

Article 9-1: Processing of Sensitive Information & Unique Identifiers

KMTR must obtain separate consent before processing sensitive data or unique identifiers (e.g., resident registration numbers).

If necessary for online registration, alternative identity verification methods (e.g., i-PIN) must be provided instead of requiring a resident registration number.

Article 10: CCTV Installation

CCTV may be installed to enhance public safety and prevent crimes following a formal approval process.

Cameras must only capture designated areas and must NOT be misused for other purposes (e.g., unauthorized monitoring of individuals).

KMTR must install notification signs stating the purpose, location, recording time, and contact details of the CCTV operator.

Article 12: Scope of Personal Information Files

KMTR maintains personal information files only within the necessary scope to fulfill its duties.

Article 13: Reporting & Modification of Personal Information Files

If KMTR intends to create, modify, or delete personal information files, it must report:

✔ File Name & Purpose

✔ Data Categories & Items Collected

✔ Collection Methods & Regular Data Providers

✔ Data Retention Period

Certain files do not require reporting, such as:

✔ National Security & Diplomacy-related files

✔ Criminal Investigation & Law Enforcement records

✔ Internal administrative records

Article 14: Maintenance of Personal Information File Register

KMTR must document and manage all personal information files except for exempted categories (e.g., security-sensitive files).

Article 15 (Ensuring the Security of Personal Information)

KMTR must take necessary measures to prevent loss, theft, leakage, alteration, or damage when processing or transmitting personal information through information networks, as defined by the Electronic Government Act.

KMTR must designate and regularly inspect restricted areas where personal information is stored, processed, and accessed.

Proper security measures must be applied to computers, storage devices, and printed documents containing personal data.

If KMTR outsources personal information processing to another public or professional organization, security measures must be implemented to prevent data breaches.

KMTR must disclose outsourcing details via its website before delegating personal information processing tasks.

Third-party processors must comply with the same security standards as KMTR.

Article 16 (Online Identity Verification)

KMTR must take appropriate measures to prevent forgery, leakage, or misuse of personal information, including resident registration numbers and names, during online verification processes.

Article 17 (Provision of Personal Information)

Definition of Personal Information Provision:

Any physical transfer (e.g., storage devices, printed materials), network transmission, or shared access that allows a third party to use personal information.

Definition of Third Party:

Any entity other than KMTR and the data subject that receives personal information, excluding service providers handling data under KMTR’s instruction.

Cases Where Third-Party Provision is Allowed:

When explicit consent is obtained from the data subject.

When legally mandated by other laws.

If the data subject cannot express consent (e.g., unconscious, missing), and the information is needed to prevent serious harm to life, health, or property.

For statistical analysis and academic research, provided the data is anonymized.

If the provision is essential to fulfill government duties and has been approved by the Personal Information Protection Committee.

For international treaty obligations (e.g., sharing with foreign governments or international organizations).

For criminal investigations and judicial proceedings.

Required Disclosures When Providing Personal Data to a Third Party:

Recipient’s name and contact information.

Purpose of data usage and specific information provided.

Retention and usage period of the data by the recipient.

Right to refuse consent and any potential disadvantages of refusal.

Security Measures When Providing Data to Third Parties:

KMTR must request the recipient to limit data use, implement security measures, and confirm compliance.

Requests for data access must be recorded and managed in a data access log.

Article 18 (Destruction of Personal Information Files)

KMTR must immediately dispose of personal information files that are no longer needed and record this in the data disposal log.

Exception: If retention is legally required, data will not be destroyed.

If personal data is disposed of, KMTR must publicly announce it on its website.

Secure disposal methods:

Electronic data must be permanently deleted using overwriting techniques to prevent recovery.

Physical documents must be shredded.

Article 19 (Obligations of Personal Information Handlers)

Employees and contractors handling personal data must not:

Disclose personal information to unauthorized parties.

Access or process data beyond their job duties.

Use personal data for unauthorized purposes.

Article 20 (Outsourcing of Personal Information Processing)

If KMTR outsources personal information processing, the following conditions must be established:

Prohibition of subcontracting.

Restrictions on copying personal data.

Monitoring and inspection of data management.

Liability for violations, including penalties for non-compliance.

KMTR must regularly inspect contractors to ensure proper data management.

 

Chapter 3: Data Subject Rights & Requests

Article 21 (Right to Access Personal Information)

Data subjects may request access to their personal information by submitting a formal request form.

KMTR must respond within 10 days unless the request falls under Article 22 restrictions. If additional time is needed, KMTR must notify the requestor of the delay.

Article 22 (Restrictions on Access Requests)

KMTR may restrict access in the following cases:

✔ When access would significantly interfere with government audits or investigations.

✔ When disclosure could harm the life, safety, or property of an individual.

Article 23 (Correction & Deletion of Personal Information)

If a data subject finds inaccuracies in their personal data, they may request corrections or deletion.

Exception: If another law requires KMTR to retain the data, deletion may be denied.

KMTR must respond promptly (within 10 days). If corrections cannot be made immediately, the requestor must be notified.

KMTR may require supporting documentation to verify the correction request.

Article 24 (Third-Party Requests & Representation)

A legal representative may request access, correction, or deletion on behalf of a data subject, provided they submit an authorization letter.
